# Privacy Policy — Neonly

_Last updated: September 2026_

Neonly does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

**What the app stores.** Neonly saves your sign text and display settings
(colour, style, speed, brightness, saved signs) locally in your own device's
browser storage. This information never leaves your device, is not sent to any
server, and is not accessible to the developer or to anyone else.

**What the app does not do.** Neonly contains no advertising, no analytics, no
tracking, no third-party software development kits, and no user accounts. It
requests no device permissions other than, optionally, keeping the screen awake
while a sign is displayed.

**Deleting your data.** Clearing the app's data from your device settings, or
using the app's own "Reset to defaults" button, removes everything Neonly has
stored.

**Children.** Neonly is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.
